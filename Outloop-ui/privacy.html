Privacy & Data

Why AccessibilityService is Used

Outloop uses Android’s AccessibilityService API strictly to inspect structural view IDs (such as reel_viewer_root) inside apps you explicitly choose, allowing it to pause short-form video feeds while keeping search and messaging usable.

Zero Data Collection Guarantee

• No keystrokes, passwords, or DM text are ever read.
• All intervention counts stay in local Room SQLite.
• Network access is restricted to read-only ETag checks for ruleset.json.
